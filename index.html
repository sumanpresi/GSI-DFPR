Report to Ministry of Mines any procurement above ₹20 cr (open/limited tender) or above ₹5 cr (negotiated, single tender, proprietary), or when an open/limited tender ends in single-source selection. Lower levels report procurements beyond their delegation to the DG for compilation at CHQ.

Chemistry/Geophysics/Engineering in-charges and HOOs have no Item 1 procurement power. “Full power” elsewhere is still capped by each authority's Item 1 limits (Condition II).
